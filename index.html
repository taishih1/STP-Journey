My Journey

2022 - 2025

2022

The Beginning

I was 11 years old and wanted to do something challenging for some random reason. The whole family decided to do it, but we hadn't done much road biking before except for my mom. We were a bit unprepared at the start and I had a not-so-great bike since we didn't know if we'd keep doing it. I don't remember much, but I remember my legs burning after day 1. They got better as we continued onto day 2. We all eventually finished, and it felt really good.

2023

Year Two

We got more serious this year. My bike upgraded and I even tried clips for the first time. It was a bit awkward at first - I fell down a few times but got used to it. As a family, we were more prepared and trained harder. The ride still felt hard, but not as challenging as the first time, being equipped with experience and training.

2024

One Day Challenge

Me and my mom attempted to do STP in one day. I was 13 and had yet another bike upgrade. We trained especially hard this time and had an experienced friend lead the pack to the finish. Yes, it was hard, but when we finished, the moment was unforgettable. The next day, I got to relax in the hotel while waiting for the rest of my family.

2025

Becoming an Ambassador

I became an STP Ambassador and got special socks. I modified another upgraded bike to have a pouch on the back filled with water and food. I rode 2-day with my sister while supporting others along the way. Not many people needed food, but I'm glad I still tried. My sister actually bonked on the road, so the food I was carrying was useful. This year was quite long because of that, but we still made it to halfway before dark. The next day, I continued trying to pass out food, then eventually rode on my own to the finish line.
